Two ways to join

Voluntary or for school credit. Your call.

Jumpstart works as a voluntary internship or as your academic internship, OJT, or practicum requirement.

Most of our interns

Voluntary adventure learning

The majority of our interns join voluntarily, on top of school, to explore, build, and get industry-ready before they graduate. No grade required, just curiosity and commitment.

  • Learn beyond the curriculum
  • Build a portfolio of real AI projects
  • Meet mentors and the industry early
Also available

Academic requirement

Need internship hours for your degree? Jumpstart can count toward your school’s internship, OJT, or practicum requirement. Tell us your school’s requirements when you apply.

  • Structured five-month program
  • Mentor supervision and documented outputs
  • Coordination with your school on requirements
The AI Engineering track

Build AI that the country can actually use

Forget the typical internship. Jumpstart focuses on your growth, with mentors who push you to see bigger opportunities in tech. On the AI Engineering track, you ship working AI for DEVCON’s nationwide community, not practice exercises.

Program structure

How the five months work

  • Flexible learning

    Asynchronous learning blended with in-person expert talks and mentorship.

  • In-person sessions

    1–2 sessions a week at the DEVCON HQ office in Makati or Ortigas, plus best-in-class conferences.

  • Exclusive tech events

    Workshops, on-site events, and career-accelerating access across the DEVCON community.

  • Guest speaker sessions

    Industry experts, each followed by an “Ask Me Anything”.

  • Transportation and communication allowance

    Provided by DEVCON throughout the internship.

FAQ

Before you apply

Is it voluntary, or can it count for school?

Both. Most of our interns join voluntarily as adventure learning, to be ready for the industry. It can also count toward your academic internship, OJT, or practicum requirement; tell us your school’s requirements when you apply.

Next stepHow to apply

Is this a paid internship?

It is unpaid, but DEVCON provides transportation and communication allowances.

Next stepSee how the internship works

What is the time commitment?

Five months of online, self-paced learning plus 1–2 in-person sessions a week, and occasional on-site and chapter events.

Next stepSee the structure

Where are the in-person sessions?

At the DEVCON HQ office in Makati or Ortigas. We’ll confirm the venue before each session. The cohort agrees on Tuesdays or Thursdays.

Next stepApply now

What do I submit?

Your CV, and a cover letter with your top 2 tracks, top 3 skills, 2–3 relevant projects, 2–3 favorite tech events, and how you learned about DEVCON.

Next stepSend your application

How does selection work?

Slots are limited and selection is competitive. Shortlisted applicants are invited for an office visit and notified within 1–2 weeks. Not shortlisted? Re-apply to a future cohort.

Next stepApply now

Which tracks are available?

Tracks depend on instructor availability and each cohort’s priorities. You pick a top 2, and you’re encouraged to collaborate on other projects.
