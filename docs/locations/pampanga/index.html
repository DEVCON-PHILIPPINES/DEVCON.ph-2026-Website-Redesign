About Pampanga

Did you know?

Pampanga’s tech community is growing fast around Clark, an emerging regional tech hub seeing growth in fintech and e-commerce. Active regional meetups make Pampanga a key contributor to Central Luzon’s tech scene.

Local economy: Pampanga is a logistics and aviation hub around Clark, plus a food-processing powerhouse. E-commerce, supply-chain tech, and digital tools for food MSMEs can help it grow.

A new chapter that shows up every month

Unique tech topics

  • Monthly meetups
  • Café community events
  • Career networking
  • Hour of AI

In its first full year, Pampanga organized 14 events and kept a steady monthly rhythm, bringing in new volunteers and students looking for knowledge and networks they can’t get in school.

A café partnership with Mug and Magic became its model for low-cost events: the chapter brings attendees, the café hosts the venue, and both win. School partners included City College of Angeles, Holy Cross Academy, and Systems Plus College Foundation.

Quality over quantity

We grow chapters, not pins on a map

A DEVCON chapter is more than a location. It needs active officers, regular local programs, and shared standards. Becoming an active chapter follows a stringent process that tests commitment, readiness, and long-term alignment with DEVCON as a non-profit, beyond seed funds and tech hype.

Limited capacity, by design. As a volunteer community, we can only support so many chapters well, and we have not renewed a total of 4 chapters for non-compliance and inactivity to date. We will always prioritize quality over quantity, of both events and impact. See chapter application requirements

First-time visitor guide

Visiting Pampanga for the first time?

Make time for these must-visit spots while you’re in town.

  • ClarkParks, food spots, and the Clark Freeport Zone.
  • San Fernando lanternsThe Giant Lantern Festival, the Christmas Capital’s pride.
  • Mount ArayatA day hike with views over Central Luzon.
  • Kapampangan food tripThe Culinary Capital: sisig, tocino, and heritage kitchens.
