John Laranga

John leads DEVCON Laguna for 2026–2027. The chapter ran 10 hands-on events across CALABARZON in 2025 and earned the Future-Ready Curriculum Award, including its first Hour of AI sessions in public schools. John keeps the focus practical, from smart contracts to microcontrollers and AI.

About Laguna

Did you know?

Laguna’s industrial parks are adopting smart manufacturing, using robotics and AI to optimize production. Local tech companies build software that improves efficiency and reduces waste, making Laguna a leader in industrial innovation.

Local economy: Laguna and CALABARZON are the country’s manufacturing belt, with economic zones for electronics and semiconductors. Automation, IoT, and Industry 4.0 skills can help local workers move up the value chain.

Quality over quantity

We grow chapters, not pins on a map

A DEVCON chapter is more than a location. It needs active officers, regular local programs, and shared standards. Becoming an active chapter follows a stringent process that tests commitment, readiness, and long-term alignment with DEVCON as a non-profit, beyond seed funds and tech hype.

Limited capacity, by design. As a volunteer community, we can only support so many chapters well, and we have not renewed a total of 4 chapters for non-compliance and inactivity to date. We will always prioritize quality over quantity, of both events and impact. See chapter application requirements

First-time visitor guide

Visiting Laguna for the first time?

Make time for these must-visit spots while you’re in town.

  • Pagsanjan FallsA boat ride through a river gorge to the falls.
  • Rizal Shrine, CalambaThe birthplace and ancestral home of Jose Rizal.
  • Los BañosHot springs and the UPLB campus at the foot of Mount Makiling.
  • Enchanted KingdomThe country’s best-known theme park in Santa Rosa.
