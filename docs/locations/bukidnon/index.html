About Bukidnon

Did you know?

Bukidnon’s agriculture is embracing smart farming, using sensors and data analytics to improve coffee and corn production and promote sustainable practices, while local developers connect farmers directly to markets.

Local economy: Bukidnon is often called the food basket of Mindanao, with pineapple, banana, corn, and highland vegetables. Precision agriculture and market platforms can help farmers earn more from every harvest.

2025 impact report

Technology with compassion, for every sector

Unique tech topics

  • BukFest
  • Agriculture and MSME tech
  • Indigenous learners
  • Women in tech
  • Sui Move

Bukidnon ran 37 events in 2025, reaching out-of-school youth, freelancers, MSME owners, students, educators, and Indigenous learners. BukFest 2025 alone gathered 6,000+ participants over a week of 12 main events, tailored for agriculture, business, education, culture, the arts, and music.

At She is DEVCON: Shertified, the chapter launched MAVIC with the GAD Office, a system that helps partner agencies monitor cases of abused women, alongside an ICP code camp. Through Bits of Light, volunteers brought tech education and care services to Indigenous learners of Luyungan Farm School, a school for children rescued from child labor and early marriage, and helped parents travel from their villages to join.

Next: adopting the school under the 2026 DEVCON Bukidnon Tech Series, “From Basics to Brilliance”, starting with teacher training so tech experts stay in the school community.

Quality over quantity

We grow chapters, not pins on a map

A DEVCON chapter is more than a location. It needs active officers, regular local programs, and shared standards. Becoming an active chapter follows a stringent process that tests commitment, readiness, and long-term alignment with DEVCON as a non-profit, beyond seed funds and tech hype.

Limited capacity, by design. As a volunteer community, we can only support so many chapters well, and we have not renewed a total of 4 chapters for non-compliance and inactivity to date. We will always prioritize quality over quantity, of both events and impact. See chapter application requirements
