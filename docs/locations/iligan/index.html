About Iligan

Did you know?

Iligan’s tech community champions cybersecurity awareness, builds collaborations through events like the Mindanao Tech Summit, and drives data science training, making Iligan an emerging player in Philippine tech.

Local economy: Iligan is known as the Industrial City of the South, home to hydropower that supplies much of Mindanao, plus steel and cement. Industrial IoT and energy tech can modernize its plants and grid.

2025 impact report

Robots, code camps, and 980 young learners

Unique tech topics

  • Robotics (mBot, micro:bit)
  • Sumo and Soccer Bots
  • Code camps
  • Code for Climate

Iligan grew fast in 2025, with a major increase in both events and volunteers across 18 events.

The chapter ran a Women’s Month mBots and micro:bits series, a Sumo and Soccer Bots Tournament with LGU Iligan, an ICT Month code camp series, a Code for Climate micro:bits series, and an outreach for 400 students at Kalamalahan Elementary School with DICT. Its 8 Hour of AI sessions reached 980 learners across 7 schools.

Quality over quantity

We grow chapters, not pins on a map

A DEVCON chapter is more than a location. It needs active officers, regular local programs, and shared standards. Becoming an active chapter follows a stringent process that tests commitment, readiness, and long-term alignment with DEVCON as a non-profit, beyond seed funds and tech hype.

Limited capacity, by design. As a volunteer community, we can only support so many chapters well, and we have not renewed a total of 4 chapters for non-compliance and inactivity to date. We will always prioritize quality over quantity, of both events and impact. See chapter application requirements
