DEVCON's pioneering programs

Built over 17 years

  • DEVCON Nationwide Chapters

    Local tech communities that connect, share knowledge, and build together through events, workshops, and meetups.

    Explore our chapters

  • DEVCON Summits

    Keynotes, workshops, hackathons, and networking that bring the country's tech community together.

    About the PRO Summit

  • DEVCON Code Camps

    Intensive, hands-on training on in-demand languages and technologies, from Move smart contracts to open-source AI.

    Read the Sui code camps case study

  • Campus DEVCON

    Workshops and events for universities and colleges, plus an Ambassador program for student leaders.

    About Campus DEVCON

  • DEVCON Kids

    Fun, hands-on computer science for young learners in underserved communities, with CSTA Philippines CPD trainings for teachers.

    About DEVCON Kids

  • #SHEISDEVCON

    Programs and trainings that equip girls and women in tech to lead.

    About #SHEISDEVCON

  • DCTX: DEVCON Community of Tech Experts

    Filipino tech experts building open-source solutions for financial inclusion and social impact.

    About DCTx

  • DEVCON for Educators

    Support programs and event collaborations that help teachers bring computer science into their classrooms.

    About DEVCON for Educators

  • DEVCON CREST

    Climate resilience technology, bringing government, business, academe, and startups together on climate challenges.

    About DEVCON CREST
