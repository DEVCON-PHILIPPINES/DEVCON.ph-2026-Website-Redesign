Our 17-year AI narrative

Seventeen years ago, a group of Filipino developers decided that technology should not be reserved for the few.

Fast forward to 2026: 13 locations, a 60,000+ strong network, and 120+ free events every year.

About us

One nationwide community. 13 locations.

DEVCON is the Philippines' largest volunteer tech community and non-profit for developers, IT enthusiasts, and future geeks. We promote the collaborative growth and global competence of Filipino developers, giving everyone from Luzon to Visayas to Mindanao a place to Sync, Support, and Succeed.

  1. 1

    Hosting unconferences for IT students, professionals, and educators since 2009, with local chapters serving as regional community hubs from Pampanga to Davao.

  2. 2

    Organizing hackathons, code camps, and innovation challenges with universities, enterprises, and government agencies.

  3. 3

    Hosting the DEVCON Summit since 2013, with sessions on open source, web3, AI, data science, and the future of work.

How we got here

Seventeen years, one idea.

  1. 2009

    The first DEVCON

    Unconferences for IT students, professionals, and educators. Free, volunteer-run, open to all.

  2. 2013

    DEVCON Summit begins

    A national stage for open source, web3, AI, data science, and the future of work.

  3. Growth

    From Luzon to Mindanao

    Chapters and volunteer communities become local tech hubs, today in 13 locations nationwide.

  4. 2025

    Builders at scale

    70k+ reached online, 19k+ face-to-face, and 800+ smart contracts deployed to mainnet.

  5. 2026

    Engineering an AI-Ready Nation

    Our 17th year puts AI skills in the hands of every Filipino builder, from kids to leaders.

The formula

Institutions aligned. The rest is ours to build.

DEVCON, Amihan and Avtica, and DOST signed an MOU to deliver the formula together.

  • Human capital

    DEVCON

    Peer learning at national scale, global certifications, and AI Certification Scholarships for the first wave of Filipino FDEs.

  • Platforms

    Amihan and Avtica

    Democratizing access to AI, so emerging nations can tap the world's best models, tools, and agents.

  • Infrastructure

    DOST

    National compute, R&D funding, and the rails for science and entrepreneurship.
