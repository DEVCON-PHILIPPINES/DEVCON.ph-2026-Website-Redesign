Case study · Campus DEVCON Summit 2023

Empowering future-ready tech leaders: 300+ student leaders, 40+ schools, one day

DEVCON’s flagship student summit, co-presented by National University and Asia Pacific College, brought IT student leaders from across Luzon together with industry leaders for keynotes, a leadership panel, and six hands-on breakout tracks.

Summit in numbers

A summit run by volunteers, for students

  • 300+Future leaders in tech on site
  • 200+HyFlex hybrid attendees
  • 90+Volunteers and organizers
  • 40+Schools represented
  • 15+ industry leader speakers
  • 6 pioneering breakout topics
  • 3 fly-in speakers from the US and India
  • 4.76 average rating
The program

Keynotes, a leadership panel, and six breakouts

The panel asked one question: what does it take to be a future-ready tech leader?

  • Keynotes

    Leading with analytics and AI, and a tech leadership journey from industry founders and executives.

  • Leadership panel

    Leaders from analytics, research, academe, and low-code on becoming future-ready.

  • Six breakout tracks

    Web development careers, cybersecurity, data science, cloud, generative AI, and business automation.
