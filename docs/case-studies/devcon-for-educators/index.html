Case study · DEVCON for Educators · with CSTA and DEVCON Kids

Training the teachers who teach computer science

Every classroom we can’t reach still has a teacher. With CSTA and DEVCON Kids, DEVCON for Educators trains teachers and adult volunteers to teach computer science and AI, so the learning keeps going long after an event ends.

Computer Science Summit

A week with the country’s science high school teachers

The second Computer Science Summit, held June 2 to 7, 2025 at the Philippine Science High School Main Campus, trained 43 CS1 and CS2 teachers from 15 PSHS campuses in teaching and learning with the BBC micro:bit, building teacher capacity to reach thousands more students.

Lead Learners

Teachers who learn first, then lead

Lead Learner workshops, in person and online, prepare teachers and adult volunteers to run Hour of AI and micro:bit sessions in their own schools.

  • 2026 Lead Learner workshops: 90 teachers in March and 90 more in May
  • AHA Sunrise: a micro:bit Lead Learner Workshop for 10 teachers
  • DICT: 120 Training Center managers trained
  • CodeAI International Facilitator Training: about 20 facilitators onboarded toward certification
On the road

From Iloilo to Mindanao

The same model powers our field work: 95 educators from 32 schools trained in AI and physical computing in Iloilo, and teachers in Bukidnon and Iligan trained in Responsible AI and Hour of AI on the Mindanao AI Caravan.
