2025 chapter story

Five high-impact events at the center

Manila kicked off 2025 with the Game On! Game Jam, then brought hands-on smart contract code camps to universities through Grassroots Blockchain in Education (DEVCON × ICP).

Elevate and Empowher: She is DEVCON × UXPH inspired hundreds of women toward leadership in development and design. The chapter spotlighted iThink Hackathon finalists at Philippine Blockchain Week, and took its mission global at WOCEE. Its first Hour of AI in the District of Manila reached 600 learners in 5 public schools.

Quick tech byte

Did you know?

Manila’s role as a major economic hub, plus a fast-growing startup scene, has drawn major international investment in FinTech and e-commerce. The result: new payment solutions and logistics advances that make Manila a key player in Southeast Asia’s digital economy.
