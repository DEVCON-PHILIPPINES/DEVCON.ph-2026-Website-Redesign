#SHEISDEVCON · Supported by the BOI

Creating a space where girls and women in tech can thrive

Bridging the gender gap in Philippine tech through mentorship, education, and community building.

Partner with #SHEISDEVCONVolunteer
  • 700+Event attendees
  • 300+Blockchain smart contract projects deployed
  • 100+Women in tech leaders engaged
Why representation matters

Close the widest STEM gender gap in Asia-Pacific

Women make up only 28% of the global tech workforce. In the Philippines, women are 36.3% of the STEM workforce, the widest STEM gender gap in the Asia-Pacific region. We’re here to change that.

  • 28%women in the global tech workforce
  • 36.3%women in the Philippine STEM workforce
  • 50%our vision for women in STEM leadership

Our mission

Empower Filipino women and girls to pursue and succeed in technology through accessible education, mentorship, and a supportive community that breaks down systemic barriers.

Our vision

A diverse, inclusive, and equitable Philippine tech industry, with women making up at least 50% of STEM leadership and workforce nationwide.
