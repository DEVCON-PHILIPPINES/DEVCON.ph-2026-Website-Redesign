DevRel case study · 2023 nationwide partnership

Low-code for every campus: a year-long nationwide program with Zoho Creator

A summer giveaway, a Business Solutions Code Camps roadshow across the country, and headline support for both DEVCON summits. One partner, one year, every channel DEVCON has.

2023 in numbers

One partnership, nationwide reach

  • 2,700+Nationwide giveaway entries
  • 26,000+Organic reach from DEVCON channels
  • 2,500+In-person reach across 10+ events
  • 140+IT students in hands-on code camps
  • 40+ schools reached
  • 25+ mentors and IT professors
  • 40+ online posts
  • 15+ media features
Phase 1 · May 15 to June 30, 2023

A Summer Giveaway that doubled as a developer survey

A points-based MacBook Air M2 giveaway ran across DEVCON’s channels and chapter events. Entrants earned points by completing Zoho Creator’s low-code survey, starting a free trial, following partner and chapter channels, answering the DEVCON Developer Survey, and signing up as volunteers.

  • 2,700+ total entries nationwide
  • 26,000+ organic reach and 40+ posts across DEVCON and chapter pages
  • 2,500+ people reached in person at 10+ events featuring the promo
  • Survey insights on how Filipino developers and IT students see low-code tools in 2023
Phase 2 · Q3 2023

Business Solutions Code Camps roadshow

Hands-on Zoho Creator code camps from Metro Manila and South Luzon to Legazpi, Iloilo, Cebu, Cagayan de Oro, Iligan, and Davao.

  • 140+ IT students

    Built working business apps on Zoho Creator in a single day.

  • 40+ schools

    Reached during the roadshow, with an average of 40 students per camp.

  • Most attendees

    Technological Institute of the Philippines sent the most, with 40 attendees.

  • Fastest finish

    De La Salle Lipa completed every code camp task by 2:00 PM.

Phase 3 · Summits

Headline support for both DEVCON summits

Zoho Creator supported the Campus DEVCON Summit 2023, with a Tech Leadership Showcase and a business automation breakout, and joined the DEVCON Pro Summit 2023 as a Gold Sponsor. The Pro Summit launch night alone drew 30+ invited media.

“I am satisfied with my experience in using Zoho Creator.”

Student participant, Campus DEVCON Summit 2023
Retrospective

What we learned for future roadshows

What worked

  • A two-week lead time per stop was enough.
  • Multi-school code camps: more attendees per stop, fewer venues to manage.
  • Pairing camps with chapter meetups and local events multiplied reach.

Where to improve

  • Hold a pre-production meeting to lock transportation and accommodations.
  • For the Visayas and Mindanao, budget for flights and visit fewer, bigger venues.
  • Start each city with a courtesy visit to the LGU.

Recommended six-day city schedule

  1. 1

    Day 1: Courtesy meeting

    Visit the LGU and local partners.

  2. 2

    Day 2: Rest and preparation

    Set up the venue and test the labs.

  3. 3

    Day 3: Multi-school code camp

    One big camp for several schools.

  4. 4

    Day 4: Travel

    Move to the next destination and rest.

  5. 5

    Day 5: Tech meetup

    Community meetup, event, or conference.

  6. 6

    Day 6: Rest

    Recover before the next stop.
