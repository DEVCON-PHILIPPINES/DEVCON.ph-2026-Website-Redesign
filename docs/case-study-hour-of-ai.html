3.8k kids introduced to AI and coding, nationwide

In 2025, DEVCON Kids brought Hour of AI and micro:bit robotics to public schools across nine chapters, with trained teachers and local volunteers in every room.

The approach

Computer science and AI for every Filipino child

Sessions pair the global Hour of AI curriculum with hands-on micro:bit robotics, taught by trained teachers and DEVCON Kids volunteers, with CPD trainings for educators through CSTA Philippines.

Chapter spotlight

DEVCON Iligan: 980 kids across 7 schools

Iligan ran 8 Hour of AI sessions across 7 schools, reaching 980 learners, plus a Sumo and Soccer Bots Tournament with the local government.

What’s next

10,000 youth in 2026

With Sui’s micro:bit and robotics kit donations and support from partners like DOST and ING, DEVCON Kids aims to empower 10,000 young Filipinos through DEVCON Kids in 2026.
