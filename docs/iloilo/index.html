About Iloilo

Did you know?

Iloilo is becoming a key player in BPO and software development. With events like Online Slingshot Region 6 at the DTI-backed Western Visayas Innovate Startup Festival, the city is pushing local tech startups to new heights.

Local economy: Iloilo pairs a fast-growing IT-BPM sector with agriculture and gastronomy; it is a UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy. Agritech, food tech, and digital services can turn that into new jobs.

2025 impact report

Investing in kids to build an ecosystem

Unique tech topics

  • AI and physical computing for educators
  • Kids coding
  • Women in tech (CoDHER)
  • Tech awards
  • Sui Move
  • Hour of AI

Iloilo ran 32 events and reached 4,833 people in 2025: 45% college students, 39% elementary and high school students, and 16% adults and professionals, its strongest professional turnout yet.

Highlights include SheIsDEVCON / CoDHER workshops for 100+ young women with DEPDEV GAD and DICT, the Tech Awards for student leaders, the GAME ON indie game competition built on Iloilo’s heritage with DOST Region 6 and the Iloilo City Government, and the four-day Tech4All Summit, run entirely through partners and volunteers.

Hour of AI #RoadTo1KYouths brought hands-on AI training to 1,680+ kids across 15 sessions in urban and rural public schools, with the Iloilo City and Provincial Governments.

Quality over quantity

We grow chapters, not pins on a map

A DEVCON chapter is more than a location. It needs active officers, regular local programs, and shared standards. Becoming an active chapter follows a stringent process that tests commitment, readiness, and long-term alignment with DEVCON as a non-profit, beyond seed funds and tech hype.

Limited capacity, by design. As a volunteer community, we can only support so many chapters well, and we have not renewed a total of 4 chapters for non-compliance and inactivity to date. We will always prioritize quality over quantity, of both events and impact. See chapter application requirements
