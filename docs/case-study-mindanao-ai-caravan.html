Case study · DEVCON AI Angat · Mindanao AI Caravan

Aral ngayon, AI trabaho bukas: Mindanao’s first AI caravan

For DEVCON’s 17th anniversary, the National Office and DEVCON Kids took free, hands-on AI and coding education on the road to Bukidnon and Iligan City. Two legs, four stops, and two tracks built for kids, teachers, students, and early-career professionals.

Partner on the next caravanMeet DEVCON Bukidnon
The caravan at a glance

Built to reach 660 learners in 8 days

Targets across both legs of the caravan.

  • 660Total attendees across the caravan
  • 240Teachers trained in Hour of AI
  • 220NEXUS morning masterclass seats
  • 120Kids in Hello, Code!
  • 60 hackathon builders
  • 40 volunteers
  • 8 days on the road
  • 8 HQ national leads co-facilitating
  • 2 host chapters
Why Mindanao

A 17th anniversary gift, and a thank-you

DEVCON AI Angat is the National Office and DEVCON Kids’ way of honoring Bukidnon and Iligan, both named Top Chapters of 2025 in Mindanao. Bukidnon is also the 2025 National Chapter of the Year.

The caravan pairs DEVCON’s national brand, curriculum, and expert facilitators with local chapter knowledge, manpower, and heart. Every session was free.

The AI empowerment journey

Meeting every learner where they are

The caravan invests in each stage, and keeps investing as learners grow.

  • Kids

    First steps in block coding, robotics, and AI at Hello, Code!

  • Teachers

    Hour of AI training, so every school keeps teaching after we leave.

  • IT and CS students

    The NEXUS AI fluency masterclass and a build-and-ship hackathon.

  • Local chapter leaders

    Sustaining the tech maker community long after the caravan leaves.

FAQ

About the caravan

Was the caravan free to attend?

Yes. Every Hello, Code! and NEXUS session was free for participants, made possible by DEVCON and its partners.

Next stepFind the next free event

Who could join Hello, Code!?

Kids and teachers pre-registered through the local chapter and partner schools.

Next stepAbout DEVCON Kids

Who could join NEXUS?

The morning masterclass was open to registered students and early-career professionals. The afternoon hackathon was limited to 30 shortlisted builders per leg.

Next stepAbout the AI Fluency Masterclass

What did participants need to bring?

Just themselves. Laptops were provided for NEXUS, and Hello, Code! kids used DepEd computing devices with MakeCode pre-installed.

How can our organization join the next caravan?
