Sponsor FAQ

What global and local partners ask us

Why partner with DEVCON Philippines?

DEVCON is the Philippines’ largest and longest-running volunteer tech community, founded in 2009, with 70k+ online reach, 19k+ face-to-face attendees in 2025, and 13 locations nationwide.

What partnership formats are available?

DevRel and code camps, summit and event sponsorship, AI Certification Scholarships and internships, DEVCON Kids donations, content and media, and in-kind or venue support.

What results can partners expect?

Verifiable outcomes and quarterly reports. For example, one DevRel code camp series produced 330+ verified mainnet submissions across five chapters in its first year.

Is DEVCON a registered non-profit?

Yes. DevConnect Philippines Inc. is an SEC-registered, BIR-compliant non-profit organization.

How do we start?

Email hello@devcon.ph. We begin with a discovery call, co-design the program and KPIs, roll it out through our chapters, and report every quarter.
