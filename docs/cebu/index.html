About Cebu

Did you know?

Cebu’s tech community is a vibrant hub: pioneering cybersecurity initiatives, partnering with events like Geeks on a Beach, and running data training programs. That makes Cebu a key player in the Philippine tech landscape.

Local economy: Cebu is a leading IT-BPM and startup hub outside Manila, with strong tourism, furniture, and shipbuilding. SaaS, cybersecurity, and AI talent can help local firms compete globally.

2025 impact report

Resilient, and the first to tackle cybersecurity

Unique tech topics

  • Cybersecurity
  • Data training
  • Geekups
  • Hour of AI

Cebu organized 15 activities and co-organized 9 more in 2025, regularizing its Geekups and running the first cybersecurity event across all DEVCON chapters.

The chapter secured an MOU with the DepEd Cebu City Schools Division for Catch Codey, its Hour of AI program, and is in talks with the Cebu Provincial Government on a Cebu tech community body. After an earthquake and two typhoons, the Hour of AI still went ahead at San Jose Elementary School once the school confirmed students were ready.

Quality over quantity

We grow chapters, not pins on a map

A DEVCON chapter is more than a location. It needs active officers, regular local programs, and shared standards. Becoming an active chapter follows a stringent process that tests commitment, readiness, and long-term alignment with DEVCON as a non-profit, beyond seed funds and tech hype.

Limited capacity, by design. As a volunteer community, we can only support so many chapters well, and we have not renewed a total of 4 chapters for non-compliance and inactivity to date. We will always prioritize quality over quantity, of both events and impact. See chapter application requirements
